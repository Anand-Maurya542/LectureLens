# Teacher & Instructor User Guide

Welcome to the **LectureLens Instructor Studio**. This guide walks you through setting up courses, ingesting lecture content, reviewing transcripts, approving assessments, and analyzing student learning.

---

## 1. Signing In as an Instructor

1. Navigate to `http://localhost:5173/login`.
2. To sign in immediately, click the demo pill: **Prof. Sharma (Instructor)**.
   - Alternatively, enter credentials: `teacher@lecturelens.edu` / `TeacherPass123!`.
3. You will be redirected to the **Instructor Studio Overview** (`/teacher`).

---

## 2. Managing Courses

1. Click **Courses** in the top navigation bar.
2. Here you see all active courses you instruct, along with enrolled student counts and published lecture tallies.
3. **Sharing Enrollment Codes**: Each course card displays a unique 6-character code (e.g. `CS401`). Click the code pill to copy it to your clipboard and share it with your students.
4. **Creating a New Course**:
   - Click **Create Course** (+).
   - Enter the Course Title (e.g., *CS 502: Advanced Deep Learning*) and an optional description.
   - Optionally provide a custom code or leave blank to auto-generate one.
   - Click **Save Course**.

---

## 3. Ingesting Lectures (Upload & YouTube)

1. Click **Add Lecture** in the navigation header.
2. Select your target course from the dropdown.
3. Enter a descriptive lecture title (e.g. *Lecture 4: Self-Attention & Transformers*).
4. Choose an Ingestion Method:
   - **Option A (YouTube Link)**: Paste the public YouTube lecture URL (e.g. `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCc8FmEb1nY`).
   - **Option B (File Upload)**: Drag and drop or browse for an audio/video recording (`.mp4`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`, etc.). Choose an optional language hint (*Auto*, *English*, or *Hindi*).
5. **Confirm Institutional Consent**: Check the recording consent verification box to confirm privacy compliance.
6. Click **Process Lecture & Generate Transcripts**.

---

## 4. Real-Time Pipeline Progress & Review Studio

Upon submission, you are redirected to the **Lecture Review Studio** (`/teacher/lectures/:id/review`):
- While processing, a pulsing amber badge **Processing AI Pipeline...** is displayed.
- The backend automatically transcribes speech, clusters topics, derives extractive summaries, builds vector chunks, and generates draft quiz questions.
- Once finished, the badge transitions to **Review Required** (Purple).

---

## 5. Reviewing & Correcting Transcripts

1. On the Review page, you have the embedded **Media Player** on the left and the **Review Tabs** on the right.
2. Click any timestamp button `[00:15 - 00:22]` to test that the player jumps to that exact moment.
3. Inspect the **Confidence Indicators**:
   - **High Confidence** (Green): High model certainty.
   - **Review Suggested** (Red): Potential technical term misrecognition or acoustic ambiguity.
4. **Inline Correction**:
   - Click the pencil icon on any segment to open the inline text editor.
   - Fix the transcription and click **Save Correction**.
   - Changes are saved, an audit trail record is created, and the vector search chunk is re-indexed immediately.

---

## 6. Reviewing Topics & Summaries

- **Topics Tab**: Review auto-detected thematic sections. Click **Edit Title** to rename sections to match your syllabus.
- **Summaries Tab**: Inspect key bullet points and representative source sentences extracted for each section.

---

## 7. Approving & Publishing to Students

By default, newly processed lectures are in **Draft / Hidden** mode to prevent students from seeing unverified materials:
- Once satisfied with the transcript and topics, click the green button: **Approve & Publish to Students**.
- The status updates to **Published** (Green).
- Enrolled students can now see the lecture in their workspace, navigate timestamps, and search its content.

---

## 8. Quiz Assessment Review Gate

1. Click **Quiz Review** in the navigation bar.
2. Select your lecture from the dropdown.
3. Review candidate multiple-choice questions generated by the AI:
   - Each question displays the prompt, answer options, correct choice, and pedagogical explanation.
   - Questions default to **Pending Review (Hidden from Students)**.
4. Click **Edit Prompt** to adjust phrasing or distractors if needed.
5. Click **Approve for Students** (Green) to release the question to students.

---

## 9. Monitoring Class Performance Analytics

1. Click **Analytics** in the navigation bar.
2. Select your course.
3. The dashboard displays:
   - Total student quiz submissions.
   - Class average score percentage.
   - **Topic Difficulty & Error Breakdown**: Ranks lecture topics by student error rates, highlighting specific concepts where students struggle so you can address them in upcoming lectures.
